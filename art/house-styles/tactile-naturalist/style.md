# Tactile Naturalist

An original naturalist-expedition visual language combining precise graphite
field sketches, hand-cut linocut silhouettes, translucent cyanotype washes,
and restrained oxidized-copper and coral-vermilion accents on warm unbleached
vellum.

Living subjects are invented evolutionary thought experiments with plausible
anatomy and adaptations. They feel carefully observed rather than cute,
heroic, or monstrous. Environments favor elemental geology, water, grasses,
mist, and distant landforms. Weather and palette communicate environmental
pressure without relying on borrowed symbols.

Composition is spacious and editorial. Keep strong focal hierarchy and broad,
calm negative space for renderer-owned text and icons. Dry-brush grain,
registration offsets, deckled edges, specimen pins, and faint measurement
grids are optional process accents used sparingly near the perimeter—not a
literal museum-board layout.

Palette: deep cyanotype blue, basalt charcoal, warm vellum, verdigris, lichen
olive, and restrained coral vermilion. Generated references contain no logos,
readable labels, component frames, game icons, existing characters, or source
game imagery.
